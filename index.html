How the filter works / statistical limitations

Each club is analyzed independently. Find the median carry and lateral offset, then scale absolute deviations by 1.4826 × median absolute deviation (MAD), with a 5-yard minimum scale for each dimension. A shot is a statistical outlier if either standardized deviation exceeds the threshold. Rank remaining shots by Euclidean distance in these two standardized dimensions; retain the selected percentage (rounded up), with at least four non-outlier shots if available. For ties, use shot order. The rest are core-trimmed, not necessarily bad shots. This is a descriptive central-cluster method, not a verified strike-quality detector. A broad or multimodal distribution can hide mishits.

Carry and lateral standard deviations use the sample formula (n−1). The expected range is the core mean ± one sample SD, not a guaranteed future-shot interval; percentiles are interpolated from the observed sample. Core statistics intentionally understate full-session variability. Use all-shot miss rates to plan conservatively. Small samples (fewer than 10 core shots) are provisional. Negative lateral = left; positive = right, assumed from the CSV convention. Face-to-path is computed as face-to-target minus club path. No handedness is assumed.
